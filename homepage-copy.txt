FAQ
Heading: Questions Authors Ask Us
What does Authors Crew do? We help authors write, edit, design, publish and market their books, all under one roof. You can use one service or let us guide you through the whole journey.
Is Authors Crew a self-publishing company? Yes. We provide professional self-publishing services. Your book is published under your own author accounts, so you keep 100% of your rights and royalties.
Which services do you offer? Ghostwriting, editing and proofreading, cover design, formatting, publishing, audiobooks and book marketing.
How are you different from other publishers? You own everything, you approve every stage, our editors are real people, and you have one project manager from start to finish. Our pricing is fixed and agreed up front.
I have an idea but haven&apos;t started writing. Where do I begin? Book a free consultation. We&apos;ll help you outline your book and recommend whether to write it yourself with editing support or work with a ghostwriter.
Do I need a ghostwriter? Not always. If you enjoy writing, our editors can help you finish and polish your manuscript. If time is short, a ghostwriter can write it with you, in your voice.
What is the easiest way to get published? With a publishing package that covers editing, design, formatting and store setup in one plan, so you don&apos;t have to manage each step yourself.
How long does publishing take? Most books are published in 3 to 8 weeks once the manuscript is ready, depending on the editing needed. Ghostwritten books take longer, and we&apos;ll give you a timeline up front.
